# DigIn

**A quiet first-person digging game about the ordinary moments of a life.**

You start in a backyard at golden hour, empty-handed. An old spade stands in a patch of softly lit soil, ringed with stones and wildflowers; nothing tells you to dig, but you want to. It plays like a digging game: dig, find minerals, sell them to the family cat who runs a little shop by the back door, buy a better spade, dig deeper. But the ground is not just ground. Buried along a hidden, winding path lie the things you once passed by without noticing: a bicycle bell, a crayon, a folded note, a house key, a coffee mug left beside your work. Each one brings back a single line of memory. Deeper earth is later life: childhood dirt, school-days clay, the hard stone of growing up. At the very bottom is today, and a blank page.

## "Let's Dig In"

- **Literally:** the whole game is digging. The earth is fully volumetric: you can dig down, sideways and through curving tunnels; loose earth falls and piles up; the ground gets harder the deeper you go.
- **Figuratively:** you are digging into a life. The layers of soil are the layers of growing up, the minerals each carry a small echo of a moment ("Quartz: clear, like the mornings before school."), and the memories are found in the order they were lived. The deeper you dig, the closer you get to today.

## What we built

- **Volumetric digging terrain:** a 48 x 40 x 48 m density field at 0.25 m resolution, meshed with marching cubes in Unity Burst jobs, split into 4 m chunks with their own mesh colliders. Each spade stroke carves a ragged scoop that opens over a few frames and crumbles at its rim.
- **Soil that behaves like soil:** after every stroke a flood fill finds earth no longer connected to the ground (and thin slivers) and drops it as falling clods; clods use the same photoscanned earth, break apart when they land hard, and settle into real heaps of soil you can walk on.
- **Feel:** an animated swing with anticipation, a 40 ms hit-stop, camera kick, a clump of earth on the blade, crumbs and dust, and a faint ring that shows where the next scoop will land.
- **A hidden life path:** an 80 m spline that winds down under the yard. Soil along it is softer and warmer; the nine memories, sixteen small memory fragments and most minerals lie along it, so players are guided without arrows or walls. The first memory lies right under the lit patch; after that, digging anywhere near an unfound memory brings up the next one in the story, and after each card a faint warm glow in the earth points the way on.
- **Finding things:** minerals come out of the scoop you just dug and rest there, glinting; you pick them up by hand (E). Memories surface in the hole; a click shows a card with an illustration and one line. The third memory of each stage brings a letter. A shelf by the back door fills with the cards, row by row: childhood, school days, growing up.
- **Economy:** eleven minerals with rarity by depth, a bag, coins, four spade tiers, bag and lantern upgrades, sold at the cat's shop: a paper price board with a rendered picture of every stone and every tier, all mouse-driven.
- **The cat:** a small Munchkin kitten who keeps the shop. It breathes and purrs, turns to watch you, licks a paw when you look away, and leans into your hand when you pet it.
- **The dream:** no other people exist, the light is a little too golden, the back door never opens, and the cat sometimes says things a cat couldn't know. After the blank page, you wake up in bed in the morning with the cat asleep on your chest, purring.
- **Look:** warm, calm, nostalgic, photorealistic: an HDRI sky, SpeedTree forest, real grass on the diggable ground, Poly Haven scans and props, URP post-processing (ACES, SSAO, bloom, warm grade, grain, vignette, depth of field).
- **Sound:** close, crisp digging right at the ear (the bite of the blade into dirt, clay or stone, and soft thuds of clods landing); long natural field-recording loops and blackbird calls in the yard that fade and muffle underground into a soft room tone and a small-room reverb; a slow, nostalgic solo piano that softens with depth and ducks under the story. All CC0 recordings, mixed quietly through an Audio Mixer.
- **Comfort:** a How to Play note on first launch, and an Esc menu like a page from an old notebook (resume, how to play, master, music and sound-effect volumes, mouse sensitivity, quit).
- **Built for judging:** a Demo mode (jump to each stage, the shop, the ending) and a Review mode that replays any story moment cleanly: each memory one scoop away, the shop with a full bag, or the whole ending.

## Built with

Unity 6.6 (URP), C#, Burst, Unity.Mathematics, the Unity CLI and Pipeline package (driving the editor), Claude Code, Meshy (3D models), Poly Haven, Freesound, OpenGameArt.

All code was written during the event. The scene is generated by an editor script (DigIn > Build Scene), so every piece of content is reproducible.
