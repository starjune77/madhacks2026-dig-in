# DigIn

**A quiet first-person digging game about the ordinary moments of a life.**

You start in a backyard at golden hour with an old spade. It plays like a digging game: dig, find minerals, sell them to the family cat who runs a little shop by the back door, buy a better spade, dig deeper. But the ground is not just ground. Buried along a hidden, winding path lie the things you once passed by without noticing: a bicycle bell, a crayon, a folded note, a house key, a coffee mug left beside your work. Each one brings back a single line of memory. Deeper earth is later life: childhood dirt, school-days clay, the hard stone of growing up. At the very bottom is today, and a blank page.

## "Let's Dig In"

- **Literally:** the whole game is digging. The earth is fully volumetric: you can dig down, sideways and through curving tunnels; loose earth falls and piles up; the ground gets harder the deeper you go.
- **Figuratively:** you are digging into a life. The layers of soil are the layers of growing up, the minerals each carry a small echo of a moment ("Quartz: clear, like the mornings before school."), and the memories are found in the order they were lived. The deeper you dig, the closer you get to today.

## What we built

- **Volumetric digging terrain:** a 48 x 40 x 48 m density field at 0.25 m resolution, meshed with marching cubes in Unity Burst jobs, split into 4 m chunks with their own mesh colliders. Each spade stroke carves a ragged scoop that opens over a few frames and crumbles at its rim.
- **Soil that behaves like soil:** after every stroke a flood fill finds earth no longer connected to the ground (and thin slivers) and drops it as falling clods; clods use the same photoscanned earth, break apart when they land hard, and settle into real heaps of soil you can walk on.
- **Feel:** an animated swing with anticipation, a 40 ms hit-stop, camera kick, a clump of earth on the blade, crumbs and dust, and a faint ring that shows where the next scoop will land.
- **A hidden life path:** an 80 m spline that winds down under the yard. Soil along it is softer and warmer; memories, sixteen small memory fragments and most minerals lie along it, so players are guided without arrows or walls.
- **Economy:** eleven minerals with rarity by depth, a bag, coins, four spade tiers, bag and lantern upgrades, sold at the cat's shop.
- **The dream:** no other people exist, the light is a little too golden, the back door never opens, and the cat sometimes says things a cat couldn't know. After the blank page, you wake up in bed in the morning with the cat asleep on your chest, purring.
- **Look:** warm, calm, nostalgic, photorealistic: an HDRI sky, SpeedTree forest, real grass on the diggable ground, Poly Haven scans and props, URP post-processing (ACES, SSAO, bloom, warm grade, grain, vignette, depth of field).
- **Sound:** soft recorded CC0 foley; birds and wind fade and muffle as you go underground, a low hum deepens with depth.

## Built with

Unity 6.6 (URP), C#, Burst, Unity.Mathematics, the Unity CLI and Pipeline package (driving the editor), Claude Code, Meshy (3D models), Poly Haven, OpenGameArt.

All code was written during the event. The scene is generated by an editor script (DigIn > Build Scene), so every piece of content is reproducible.
